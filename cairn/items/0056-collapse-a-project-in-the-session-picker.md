## Problem

The picker lists every session under every project. With enough projects that becomes a wall, even grouped.

## Proposal

Fold a project to its caption line, remembering which are folded between runs.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A key folds and unfolds the project under the cursor
- [ ] A folded project still shows its session count and whether any are live
- [ ] The fold survives closing and reopening the picker
